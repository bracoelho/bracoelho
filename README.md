### What I am building

**[NEWS-Radar](https://radar.bcoelho.com)** reads several hundred AI items a day
from research feeds, vendor announcements, developer communities and trending
repositories, then publishes the few that would change a decision. Each one
carries the reasoning: what it changes, for whom, and what would have to be true
for it to matter.

Five themes, each with its own bar: critical infrastructure, reliability and
assurance, business and markets, practice, and research worth watching. The
[method](https://radar.bcoelho.com/method/) explains how the selection works.

### What I write about

Autonomy and social licence, and why deployment is gated by the risk a society
accepts long after the technology is ready.

The distance between how an AI system behaves when it is evaluated and how it
behaves in production, which is a governance problem before it is a research
problem.

Where human judgement stays non-negotiable inside AI workflows: defining what
good means, choosing which directions to explore, and knowing when the work is
finished.
